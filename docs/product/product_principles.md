# Product Principles

These core principles guide all product decisions for StoryTree.

## 1. Ideas Over Authors
We organize content around prompts (ideas) rather than authors. Prompts are the seeds; stories are the branches. The platform is designed to celebrate creative interpretations of the same idea.

## 2. Creative Collaboration
Instead of isolation, writers participate in a shared universe. One prompt can spawn hundreds of unique stories, creating a collaborative "tree" of creativity.

## 3. Emotional Reactions, No Downvotes
We prioritize constructive, emotional feedback. There are no downvotes to discourage writers. Instead, readers react with emotional tokens: 🌟 Star, ❄️ Chills, 🤯 Mind Blown, ❤️ Moved, 😂 Hilarious, 🌸 Beautiful.

## 4. Quality Reading Experience
The interface must optimize for sustained reading. Typography, spacing, and colors must prevent eye strain and create an immersive environment.

## 5. Discoverability
The recommendation engine optimizes for creative satisfaction (stories finished + reacted to positively), not just time spent on the platform.
